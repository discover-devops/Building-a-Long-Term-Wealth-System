##  Step-by-Step Tutorial Outline

###  **Part 1: Mindset & Philosophy**

* **Goal** → Wealth creation through stocks (Target example: ₹10 Cr by 2030)
* **Principle** → Focus on Business Quality, Growth, and Risk Management
* **Approach** → Build multiple “stock engines”:

  * Long-term compounders
  * Growth stocks
  * Momentum plays
  * Early-stage bets

> *“You cannot succeed by copying others. Learn principles and discover your own process.”*

---

###  **Part 2: Stock Investing Structure**

| Portfolio Engine          | Purpose                            | % Allocation |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------ |
| **Super Stocks (2035)**   | Safe, consistent compounders       | 50–70%       |
| **Breakout Radar**        | Short to medium-term growth trades | 10–20%       |
| **Emerging Multibaggers** | High-risk, high-reward smallcaps   | 10–15%       |
| **Growth Gems**           | Mid/large-cap quality growth       | 10–20%       |

> *“Don’t put all eggs in one strategy. Diversify by business type, not just sectors.”*

---

###  **Part 3: Screener Development**

**A. Super Stocks Screener (2035)**

* ROCE > 25
* Sales growth > 12%
* Market cap > ₹10,000 Cr
* Piotroski score > 6

**B. Breakout Radar**

* ROCE & ROE > 18%
* Sales & Profit Growth (YoY) > 15–20%
* Debt to equity < 0.5
* Price to Earnings < 40

**C. Special Emerging Stocks Screener**

* Market cap ₹500–₹5000 Cr
* 5Y Sales & Profit Growth > 15%
* ROE & ROCE > 15%
* Low debt, high promoter holding

**D. Growth Gems Radar**

* PEG < 2
* Strong sales & EPS growth
* Low debt, high ROCE

**E. Institutional Growth Radar**

* FII + DII Holding increasing > 10% (3 years)
* Growth, ROCE, Debt, Promoter filters

> *“Each screener serves a specific purpose. Use them wisely, not emotionally.”*

---

###  **Part 4: Stock Review Checklist (Rahul’s 10 Questions)**

For every stock shortlisted, ask:

1. What does the company do?
2. Is the sector growing?
3. Is revenue growing 5+ years?
4. Are margins stable or improving?
5. Is debt low?
6. Is promoter holding strong?
7. Any red flags?
8. Is the company profitable consistently?
9. What’s the obvious risk?
10. Will I stay calm if stock drops 30%?

> *“Screeners give you quantity. Your questions give you quality.”*

---

###  **Part 5: Review Routine**

| Frequency           | Task                                 |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **Weekly (Sunday)** | Check Breakout Radar + FnO           |
| **Monthly**         | Run Growth Gems & Emerging Screeners |
| **Quarterly**       | Review Super Stocks                  |
| **Study**           | 2–3 new companies per month          |

> *“Consistency beats intensity. Review, refine, repeat.”*

---

###  **Part 6: Core Discipline Rules**

* Don’t chase every stock.
* Study before buying.
* Don’t overtrade.
* Stick to allocation plan.
* Embrace patience.
* Review quarterly, not daily.

> *“Your system must be stronger than your mood.”*
